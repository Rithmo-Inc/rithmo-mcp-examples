# Connect a ChatGPT agent

**Before you start:** custom connectors need Developer mode on ChatGPT Plus and Pro. On Business and
Enterprise, a workspace admin enables them.

1. In Rithmo, open **Connections** and choose **Connect** on the ChatGPT card. Copy the MCP URL it shows.
2. In ChatGPT, open the connector settings and add a custom MCP server:
   - **Name:** Rithmo
   - **URL:** the MCP URL from step 1
   - **Authentication:** OAuth
3. Sign in with your Rithmo account and approve the connection.
4. Turn on the Rithmo connector for the agent.

If you added Rithmo before, reconnect the existing connector instead of adding a second one.
